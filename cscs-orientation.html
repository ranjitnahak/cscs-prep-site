Doubts — How To Clarify

Got a Doubt? Here's Where It Goes

"
Every question — a concept, a quiz item, an assignment task — goes into the WhatsApp group. That's the one place to ask.
  • 01Post your doubt in the WhatsApp group, anytime
  • 02Keep it in the group, not DM — others learn from it too
  • 03Bigger doubts get cleared in the Live Session
  • 04Technical or access issues (login, payment) → message the CSCSPrep team directly, not the group
Important Pointer

Zero Follow-Up System

We will not chase you to complete chapters, quizzes, or assignments. Your progress is entirely in your own hands.

  • No reminder calls
  • No nagging messages
  • Chapters stay unlocked and waiting for you, whenever you're ready
